## ![sunrise](/img/sunrise/0.jpg)

How well does this represent the current state of the sky for you? 

This isn't mapped at all to accurately represent the sky during the time of day in any given locale (I'll maybe™ build something out using dayJS to better do this in the future). It uses a pretty simple script that cycles through an array of 24 images (technically 10 images that are repeated/ reversed at different parts of the cycle) depending on the time of day.

I have to remind myself to open the curtains every now and then, otherwise this progression (the uh... I guess the literal progression of time...) is totally lost on me. If you need it, here's a reminder for you as well.
