We've been fully remote for little over 2 months now at the MaxRewards (no-longer) office. Our office lease has been cancelled (no use for it really since the **world is ending**) and we all have proper work-from-home setups going.

All in all we're still going to keep marching towards MaxRewards V2, funded or not, because even though nobody is spending as much irl, people still need to be told that they should get the Amazon Prime card because they're spending half of their income on that website.

## Exposure

I actually got COVID in early April. It was a pretty awful experience; I was essentially bedridden for the better part of a week and spent the week after in what I can only describe as a fugue state wrapped in a nightmare.

Really not sure how people aren't taking this seriously, but then that would require ignoring the id (or is it the ego?) for the humble common good. That sounds like it could only lead to bad things, right?
