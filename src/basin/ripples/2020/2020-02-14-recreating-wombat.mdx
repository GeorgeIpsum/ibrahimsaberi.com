## A Note on Wombats

Wombats are marsupials native to Australia. They are known for their burrowing habits and their distinctive cube-shaped feces. They have stumpy little legs and are not at all related to bats despite the namesake. They look kind of like if guinea pigs and koalas had really cute babies. The "combat wombat" as described in this article is a non-fictional[^fiction] concept, and all information related to combat wombats is based on highly empirical evidence and multiple peer-reviewed studies. The author of this article was not responsible for any wombat related injuries or misinformation being spread at the time of its publishing.

[^fiction]: as in real. not fake.

In this article, we will explore the ethical implications of using wombats in combat scenarios, as well as the challenges of recreating wombats in a digital environment. We'll also explore the technical aspects of simulating wombats using close-to-realistic blast physics a la Fire Blast.
