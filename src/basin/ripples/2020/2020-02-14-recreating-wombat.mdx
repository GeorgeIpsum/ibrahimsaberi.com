## A Note on Wombats

Wombats are marsupials native to Australia. They are known for their burrowing habits and their distinctive cube-shaped feces. They have stumpy little legs and are not at all related to bats[^etym] despite the namesake. They look kind of like if a guinea pig, koala, and a badger had really cute babies.[^try_this]

The "combat wombat" as described in this article is a non-fictional[^fiction] concept, and all information related to combat wombats is based on highly empirical evidence and multiple peer-reviewed studies. The author of this article was not responsible for any wombat related injuries or misinformation being spread at the time of its publishing.
